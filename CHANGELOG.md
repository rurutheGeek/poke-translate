## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-27

First public release.

- Term protection: official names for Pokémon, moves, abilities, items, types,
  natures, stats, locations and forms in nine languages, from PokéAPI.
- Overseas notation: `Landorus-Therian`, `Water-types`, `Tera Steel`,
  `Psychic immunity`, `Atk / SpD / Spe`, `OHKO`, `STAB` and other battle terms.
- Showdown sets are converted term by term without machine translation.
- Static translation site and a Manifest V3 browser extension (bilingual view,
  translated-only view, auto-translation of content added later).
